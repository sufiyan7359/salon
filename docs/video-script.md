# Video Walkthrough Script / वीडियो वॉकथ्रू स्क्रिप्ट

Use this as narration if you record your own screen-capture demo (e.g. with QuickTime Screen Recording on Mac, or any phone screen recorder). Suggested total length: ~4-5 minutes. Each line is what to say while you perform that action on screen.

Agar aap khud screen-record karke demo video banana chahte hain (jaise Mac par QuickTime Screen Recording, ya kisi bhi phone screen recorder se), toh isko narration ke roop mein use karein। Suggested total length: ~4-5 minute। Har line woh hai jo aapko screen par woh action karte waqt bolna hai।

---

## Part 1 — Customer side (~2.5 min) / भाग 1 — कस्टमर साइड

**[Screen: open the salon link on your phone]**

- EN: "This is the salon's page. You can see our services, our team, and reviews from other customers. Right now it shows how many people are waiting."
- HI: "यह हमारे सैलून का पेज है। यहां आप हमारी सर्विसेज़, टीम, और दूसरे ग्राहकों के रिव्यू देख सकते हैं। अभी यह दिखा रहा है कि कितने लोग वेट कर रहे हैं।"

**[Tap "Join Queue"]**

- EN: "To join the queue, I just tap 'Join Queue' and enter my mobile number."
- HI: "क्यू में जुड़ने के लिए, मैं बस 'Join Queue' पर टैप करता हूं और अपना मोबाइल नंबर डालता हूं।"

**[Enter phone, tap Send OTP, show OTP screen]**

- EN: "I get a 6-digit code by SMS. I enter it here, and I can also add my name."
- HI: "मुझे SMS से 6 अंकों का कोड मिलता है। मैं उसे यहां डालता हूं, और चाहूं तो अपना नाम भी डाल सकता हूं।"

**[Choose services, tap Continue, show confirm screen]**

- EN: "Now I pick the service I want — and I can even choose my favorite staff member. It shows me the total price and time before I confirm."
- HI: "अब मैं वो सर्विस चुनता हूं जो मुझे चाहिए — और चाहूं तो अपना पसंदीदा स्टाफ भी चुन सकता हूं। कन्फर्म करने से पहले यह मुझे कुल कीमत और समय दिखाता है।"

**[Tap Join Queue, land on tracking page]**

- EN: "And that's it — I'm in the queue. This screen updates on its own, live — my token number, how many people are ahead of me, and my estimated wait time, which keeps counting down."
- HI: "बस, हो गया — मैं क्यू में आ गया। यह स्क्रीन खुद-ब-खुद, लाइव अपडेट होती है — मेरा टोकन नंबर, मुझसे कितने लोग आगे हैं, और मेरा अनुमानित वेट टाइम, जो लगातार कम होता रहता है।"

**[Navigate back to home page, point at the banner]**

- EN: "Even if I go back to the home page, my token still shows right at the top — so I don't have to keep searching for the link."
- HI: "अगर मैं वापस होम पेज पर भी जाऊं, तब भी मेरा टोकन ऊपर ही दिखता रहता है — इसलिए मुझे बार-बार लिंक ढूंढने की जरूरत नहीं।"

**[Mention SMS reminder — can be spoken over the tracking screen, no live demo needed unless you want to wait]**

- EN: "And when my turn is about 10 minutes away, I get a text message telling me to head to the salon — so I don't have to sit staring at my phone the whole time."
- HI: "और जब मेरी बारी लगभग 10 मिनट दूर होती है, मुझे एक SMS मिलता है जो बताता है कि सैलून पहुंच जाऊं — इसलिए मुझे पूरे समय फोन पर घूरते रहने की जरूरत नहीं।"

**[Show the review card, if you have a completed entry to demo with]**

- EN: "Once my visit is done, I can rate my experience right here."
- HI: "जब मेरी विजिट पूरी हो जाती है, तो मैं यहीं अपना अनुभव रेट कर सकता हूं।"

**[Tap the EN/हि toggle]**

- EN: "And the whole app works in both English and Hindi — I just tap this toggle anytime."
- HI: "और यह पूरी ऐप English aur Hindi dono mein kaam karti hai — main kabhi bhi yeh toggle tap kar sakta hoon।"

## Part 2 — Owner side (~2 min) / भाग 2 — ओनर साइड

**[Open owner login, sign in]**

- EN: "Now let's look at the owner side. I sign in with my email and password."
- HI: "अब ओनर साइड देखते हैं। मैं अपने ईमेल और पासवर्ड से साइन इन करता हूं।"

**[Show Live Queue screen]**

- EN: "This is my main screen — everyone currently waiting, in order. If someone walks in without the app, I can add them here as a walk-in."
- HI: "यह मेरी मुख्य स्क्रीन है — जो भी अभी वेट कर रहे हैं, order mein। Agar koi bina app ke seedha aa jaaye, main unhe yahan walk-in ke roop mein add kar sakta hoon।"

**[Tap Call Next on a customer]**

- EN: "When I'm ready for the next customer, I tap 'Call Next.' Once I'm done with them, I mark them Complete — and everyone behind them moves up automatically, live, on their phones too."
- HI: "Agle customer ke liye ready hote hi, main 'Call Next' tap karta hoon। Unka kaam hone ke baad, main unhe Complete mark karta hoon — aur unke peeche wale sab automatically aage badh jaate hain, live, unke phone par bhi।"

**[Mention the arriving-soon toast]**

- EN: "And I also get notified right here whenever a waiting customer's turn is about 10 minutes away — so I always know who's about to arrive."
- HI: "Aur mujhe bhi yahin pata chal jaata hai jab kisi waiting customer ki bari lagbhag 10 minute door ho — isse mujhe hamesha pata rehta hai kaun aane wala hai।"

**[Click through Services, Staff, Bookings, Analytics tabs briefly]**

- EN: "From the sidebar, I can manage my services, my staff, confirm advance bookings, and check my analytics — how many customers I've served, my estimated revenue, and my ratings."
- HI: "Sidebar se main apni services, apna staff manage kar sakta hoon, advance bookings confirm kar sakta hoon, aur apni analytics check kar sakta hoon — kitne customers serve kiye, anumanit kamai, aur ratings।"

**[Tap the EN/हि toggle]**

- EN: "And just like the customer side, my whole dashboard also switches between English and Hindi with one tap."
- HI: "Aur customer side ki tarah, mera poora dashboard bhi ek tap mein English aur Hindi ke beech switch ho jaata hai।"

## Recording tips / रिकॉर्डिंग टिप्स

- Record the customer side on your phone (or phone screen mirrored to a computer); record the owner side on a laptop/desktop for the sidebar layout.
- Keep each screen on-screen for 3-4 seconds before speaking, so viewers can actually read it.
- Do two passes if it's easier: one full pass narrating in English, one in Hindi — instead of switching languages mid-recording.
- Har screen ko 3-4 second tak dikhayein bolne se pehle, taaki viewers usko padh sakein।
- Agar aasan lage toh do baar record karein: ek pura pass English mein, ek Hindi mein — beech mein language switch karne ke bajaye।
